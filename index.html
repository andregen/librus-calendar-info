Librus calendar

Keep school timetables in dedicated Google calendars.

What this application does

This private, owner-operated integration reads linked children's lesson timetables from Librus and synchronizes dated lessons to separate calendars it creates in the owner's Google account. It is not affiliated with Librus or Google and is not a public account service.

The integration is being configured. Scheduled synchronization is not yet enabled.

Google access

The app requests permission to create and manage its own secondary calendars and their events, and to read the list of calendars to avoid duplicates. It does not request access to the contents of unrelated calendars.
